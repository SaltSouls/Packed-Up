## Distribution Rules:

### What You Can Do Without Asking:
- **Redistribute the mod.** You may share the unmodified Packed Up jar, including as part of a modpack. Modpacks may earn platform rewards (such as CurseForge or Modrinth rewards), but a modpack that includes Packed Up must not be paywalled. That means no requiring payment, a subscription, or an early-access purchase to download it.
- **Make resource packs.** You may create and distribute resource packs, free or paid, that contain substantially edited versions of Packed Up's textures and models. Please credit Packed Up in your pack's description.

### What You Can't Do:
- **Include unedited assets in resource packs.** Resource packs may not include Packed Up's original assets as-is. They never need to: any texture or model a pack doesn't replace is loaded from Packed Up automatically.
- **Use the assets in other projects without permission.** Using Packed Up's assets, as-is, in another mod or project requires written permission from me (see below).
- **Sell the assets or claim them as your own.** You may not sell Packed Up's assets or present them as your own, or someone else's work.

### Asking for Permission:
- **Preferred:** open an issue on GitHub at https://github.com/SaltSouls/Packed-Up/issues
- **Also accepted:** send a message to me on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/members/twinsouls).
